Model Editor V11 - what changed (2026-11-14, Solomon's notes)

LAYERS ARE EDITABLE. In a 2D paper view, every row of the parts
list has move-toward-front and move-toward-back buttons, and
Save writes the new layer order into the rig file as
layer_order_back_to_front. In the 3D view, a Layers panel lists
every page of the loaded model front-first, grouped by the
axis its stack runs on, with the same buttons - the pages swap
depths live, so the neck can be moved behind the face by hand.

PAPER RIG POINTS MOVE ON X AND Y ONLY. Points that come from a
paper GLB (they ride inside the file) drag in the flat X/Y
plane and their panel shows X and Y sliders only - no Z - so a
paper rig stays flat and works properly with paper. Points of
a true 3D model keep all three axes.

THE MODEL LOADS IN THE CENTRE. When a GLB opens, the camera
and the orbit target move to the model's own centre, at a
distance that fits the whole model in the window.

LIGHT MODE REACHES THE 3D STAGE. The background behind the
model follows the app's appearance - light in light mode,
dark in dark mode - for the model view and the paper stack
preview alike.

Everything from V10 stands: the editor opens empty, a rigged
GLB carries its points inside it, support files live beside
the app and never inside it.

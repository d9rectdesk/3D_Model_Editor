Model Editor V16 - what changed (2026-11-15, mobile touch)

TOUCH AND SMALL SCREENS. The editor now works on a
mobile touch screen. V15 already had the viewport tag
and dragged by pointer events; V16 adds the style rules
that make touch behave and the layout fit.

- Touch drags move points and views instead of
  scrolling the page: touch-action none on the 2D
  stage, the 3D stage and its canvas, and every rig
  point dot. The body and the controls use manipulation,
  so taps are never delayed or double-tap-zoomed.
- On screens 860px wide and below the layout stacks:
  the stage takes the full width on top, the side panel
  moves below it and scrolls its own length, and the
  toolbars wrap. Nothing needs sideways scrolling.
- On coarse pointers (touch), buttons, menus, and list
  rows get a 44px tap height, slider thumbs grow to
  30px, and each rig point's hit area grows to a 28px
  radius through a clear halo - the drawn dot itself
  stays 26px, exactly as on a fine pointer.

Everything from V15 stands unchanged: every element id
(75 by the standing census), every feature, the tested
angle ranges, the 3D movement player, and the editor
still opens empty with no model inside it. Proof is
code-level only; no real device has touched it yet.

Model Editor V13 - what changed (2026-11-15, Solomon's design)

3D MOVEMENT. The 3D view gains a movement player, built the
way Solomon described it: the model's points keep the same
2D placement, and in 3D they stand on a line perpendicular
to the rest. A wave runs down the chain of points, each
point following the one before it by its depth in the
chain.

- Snake: the wave travels along the perpendicular, so the
  chain ripples in depth like a snake.
- Spiral: the wave circles around the perpendicular, so
  the chain corkscrews like a spiral.
- Amount and Speed sliders set how far and how fast.
- Stop puts every point back on its exact placed position.

The points themselves are not changed by playing - the
movement is a preview over the placed rig, and saving
still writes the placed points. Stop the movement before
dragging points.

Everything from V12 stands: the views are separate, the 2D
views open, the Layers panel is readable, points sit on
their pages, and the Chrome tuning is in.

Model Editor V12 - what changed (2026-11-14, Solomon's report)

THE VIEWS ARE SEPARATE. The human man is no longer assembled
into one overlapped figure. His front, back, and side stand
apart as three groups, and each is edited on its own - in the
picker as its own 2D paper view, and in the 3D view as its own
figure standing clear of the others.

THE 2D VIEWS OPEN AGAIN. Switching the picker from the 3D view
to a 2D view showed nothing: the view data from the GLB was
missing one list the parts builder needs, and the builder died
after clearing the stage. That is fixed, the builder no longer
depends on the list being present, and if a view ever fails to
build, the status line now says why instead of going blank.

THE LAYERS PANEL IS READABLE. It was a wall of bare buttons -
the layer names took the page's dark text colour onto the dark
panel, and the whole list had no scroll box, so it grew past
the screen. Names are now light and readable, grouped by
stack, inside their own scroll area.

POINTS SIT ON THEIR PAGES. Rig points are placed through each
page's own position in the model, so the side view's points
land on the side figure instead of floating beside it.

CHROME TUNING. Both 3D renderers ask Chrome for the
high-performance GPU and cap their pixel ratio at 2; the 3D
view renders only while it is on screen; and when a new model
replaces an old one, the old model's GPU memory is released.

Everything from V11 stands: the editor opens empty, layers
can be reordered, paper points move on X and Y only, the
model loads centred, and the stage follows light and dark.

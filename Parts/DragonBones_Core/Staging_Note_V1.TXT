WHAT IT DOES FOR THE RIG WORK
A layered figure's parts are rigid paper pieces parented to
bones. This core is the evaluator: it parses a skeleton
description, builds the bone hierarchy, and evaluates every
bone's and slot's pose for any joint angle - which is what
the movement proof consumes when it poses each part through
its range and checks that no background can show. It authors
nothing and renders nothing; the editor keeps its own
surfaces. Its engine adapters (Pixi, Phaser, Egret and the
rest) are NOT staged - the editor supplies its own thin
display factory, exactly as the proof harness does.

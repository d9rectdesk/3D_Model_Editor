Model Editor V19 - what changed (2026-11-15, the
Paper Doll Plan's final stitch)

V19 adds one thing, and it is the whole paper doll
pipeline: a new panel section, "Paper doll - build from
three pictures". One pick of the man's front, back, and
side pictures, and the editor runs Bits 1 through 5 of
the Paper Doll Plan itself, unattended, saying what it
is doing in its own log while it works:

Bit 1 - it cuts the three pictures out clean (the
border-flood cut of Cut_Clean_Cutouts_V1.PY, ported).
Bit 2 - it splits each cutout into the doll's parts
(nearest bone, the shorts as the pelvis, the head
carrying the neck, face features carved, stray islands
handed to their neighbours - Split_Into_Parts_V1.PY,
ported): Front 19, Back 16, SideRight 17, SideLeft 17.
Bit 3 - it completes every part under its neighbours
and grows a buffer disc or capsule of the part's own
substance at every joint, the pelvis filling alone
under the clothing (Complete_And_Buffer_V1.PY, ported).
Bit 4 - it writes the rig points into the flat paper
GLB, every point with its pin, parent, label, and
written description (Build_Paper_Doll_GLB_V1.PY,
ported).
Bit 5 - it assembles the four views into the 3D paper
model at the origin (Build_Paper_Doll_3D_V1.PY, ported)
and exports that GLB.

Then it generates the walk page from that GLB (the
Bit 6 page pattern, its template carried inside the
editor) and downloads three things: the 3D GLB, the
walk page, and the full stitch set - cutouts, split
parts, completed parts, the rig, the census, both
GLBs, and the walk page - so the plan's battery can be
re-run on the editor's own outputs.

Proof: the stitch battery, re-run end to end on the
stitched product's own outputs by the bits' own
instruments - Bit 1: background inside silhouette 0 px
and white fringe 0 px on all three cutouts. Bit 2:
census exact, every part one connected piece, partition
overlap 0, missing 0, extra 0 in all four views. Bit 3:
the pose battery and the per-joint sweep count zero
background pixels inside any joint's disc in all four
views, rest coverage missing 0. Bit 4: the editor
round-trip on the stitch GLB in this editor - 24
passed, 0 failed. Bit 5: 16 passed, 0 failed, every
render equal to its own view's assembly pixel for
pixel. Bit 6: the generated walk page driven in a real
engine - 23 passed, 0 failed, heading sweeping 360
degrees, radius constant, four quarter stills showing
four facings, contact sheet saved. And this editor's
own harness: 39 passed, 0 failed - every V18 check
passes as it did; nothing was removed, three elements
were added (dollRun, dollPick, dollLog).
